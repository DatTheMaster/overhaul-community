## Themes

### Corporate Mode — the endgame tier

The long-term endgame: the wealthiest operators found corporations that generate
specialty freight, with the truck market and the investment layer built on real
delivered profit. Designed as a phased rollout — nothing ships before it is proven.

- [ ] **Phase A — cargo specialties & specialty trucks** (#19) — the box truck gets
      real specializations (general / cold box), with premium pay for specialized hauls
- [ ] **Inter-hub stock transfer** (#12) — the hub-and-spoke loop: stock moves between
      a founder's own hubs with the large fleet, local distribution with the small
      vehicles
- [ ] **Active-port rotation** (#24) — ports rotate through active status as ships
      depart, opening new routes (Istanbul and beyond)

### Transport expansion

- [ ] **Ships, planes, the broader logistics network** (#130) — extending freight
      beyond the road network
